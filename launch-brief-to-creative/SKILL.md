# Brief to creative to copy / 从需求书到创意与文案

Use this skill when the work starts with a launch brief, stakeholder comments, product material, or a draft proposal and needs to become a coherent event concept and slide-ready content. It covers thinking and writing, not fabrication of product evidence.

从需求书、相关方反馈、产品资料或方案草稿出发，需要形成连贯的活动创意和可直接进演示文稿的内容时使用。本技能处理分析与写作，不编造产品证据。

## 1. Diagnose the brief / 诊断需求

- Extract the audience, business goal, product proof points, mandatory moments, schedule, venue constraints, budget band, decision makers, and known prohibitions. Record the source and status of each item: confirmed, proposed, or unknown. / 提取目标人群、商业目标、产品证据、必做环节、时程、场地约束、预算范围、决策方与已知禁区，并标记来源与状态：已确认、建议、待确认。
- Rewrite vague requests as answerable questions. Ask only questions whose answers change the creative direction, feasibility, claim accuracy, or authorization. Continue work that does not depend on those answers. / 把模糊要求改写为可回答的问题。只问会改变创意方向、可执行性、事实准确性或授权边界的问题；不依赖答案的工作继续推进。
- Distinguish direct feedback from internal inference. A negative comment does not prove an entire direction was rejected unless that decision was stated. / 区分客户原话与内部推断。负面评价不自动等于整条方向已被否决，除非有明确决定。

## 2. Build the creative logic / 搭建创意逻辑

Use one traceable chain for each proposed highlight:

`Audience question → product capability or evidence → live action → visual/spatial expression → audience takeaway`

每个亮点按可追溯的链条建立：

`用户问题 → 产品能力或证据 → 现场动作 → 视觉/空间表达 → 观众记忆点`

Choose a central proposition that can explain the event flow, stage, display, and communications without forcing every detail into one metaphor. Test it against the most important product evidence and the real audience. If it fails either test, revise the proposition before expanding visuals.

选择能够解释流程、舞台、展区和传播内容的核心主张，不必把每个细节都塞进同一个比喻。用关键产品证据和真实受众需求检验；若无法解释，先调整主张，再延展视觉。

## 3. Turn logic into content / 从框架到内容

- Make the rundown causal: opening raises the question, subsequent sections prove capabilities, the high point synthesizes evidence, and the ending gives a clear action or conclusion. Keep timing and speaker ownership visible. / 流程要有因果：开场提出问题，后续模块证明能力，高点汇总证据，结尾给出清晰行动或结论。标明时长与讲述责任。
- For every live moment, specify the trigger, who acts, what the audience sees, what evidence appears, and how the moment resolves. Distinguish stage action from screen content and from an edited video. / 每个现场高点明确触发方式、执行者、观众所见、出现的证据和收束方式。区分真人舞台动作、屏幕内容与预制视频。
- Anchor comparisons and demonstrations to approved test conditions. Do not turn a visual metaphor or rehearsed result into an unqualified factual claim. / 对比与演示以获准测试条件为依据；不要把视觉比喻或彩排效果写成无条件事实结论。
- Identify the production dependency for each effect: media content, tracking, lighting, live camera, physical prop, rehearsal, safety, or fallback. / 标出每个效果的制作依赖：媒体内容、追踪技术、灯光、实时摄像、实体道具、彩排、安全或备选方案。

## 4. Polish copy and process feedback / 打磨文案与反馈

- Lead with the point. Use short titles that reveal the action or conclusion, followed by one sentence explaining what happens and why it matters. Prefer concrete verbs and observable outcomes to generic excitement. / 先说结论。标题点明动作或结果，再用一句话说明发生什么、为何重要。优先使用具体动词和可观察结果，少写空泛情绪词。
- Match the requested length and register: one-line conclusion, slide copy, spoken script, or production note are different deliverables. Keep technical detail where it supports a decision. / 根据需求区分一句话结论、PPT文案、口播稿和执行说明。技术细节只在有助于决策时出现。
- Log each revision as `feedback → interpretation → changed page or moment → unresolved decision`. Preserve previous versions. / 每轮按“反馈 → 判断 → 修改的页面或环节 → 未决事项”记录，保留旧版本。
- Before external sharing, remove identifiable client, brand, product, people, artwork, commercial terms, private paths, and unreleased claims. / 对外分享前，移除可识别客户、品牌、产品、人物、画面、商务条款、私有路径和未发布信息。
